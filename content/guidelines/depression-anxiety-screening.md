## Who it applies to

Adults aged 18 and older, including pregnant and postpartum people, who have no diagnosed mental health condition.

## Depression

Screen all adults for depression (grade B). Screening should be paired with systems for accurate diagnosis, effective treatment and follow-up. Common tools include the PHQ-2 and PHQ-9.

## Anxiety

Screen adults aged 64 and younger for anxiety disorders, including pregnant and postpartum people (grade B). The evidence is insufficient for adults 65 and older (I statement). The GAD-7 is a commonly used tool.

## Suicide risk

The evidence is insufficient to assess screening for suicide risk in adults who have no symptoms (I statement). Anyone in crisis in the United States can call or text 988.
