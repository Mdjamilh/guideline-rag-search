## Who it applies to

Adults aged 18 and older without known hypertension.

## Recommendation

Screen for high blood pressure with office measurement (grade A). Before starting treatment, the diagnosis should be confirmed with measurements taken outside the clinical setting, such as ambulatory blood pressure monitoring or home blood pressure monitoring.

## How often

Screen every year for adults aged 40 and older and for younger adults at increased risk, such as those with high-normal blood pressure, overweight or obesity, or Black adults. Adults aged 18 to 39 with normal blood pressure and no other risk factors can be rescreened every 3 to 5 years.

## Notes

Confirming with out-of-office readings helps avoid overdiagnosis from white coat hypertension.
