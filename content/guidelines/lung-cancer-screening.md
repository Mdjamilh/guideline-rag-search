## Who it applies to

Adults aged 50 to 80 who have a smoking history of at least 20 pack-years and who either currently smoke or quit within the past 15 years. A pack-year is one pack of cigarettes a day for one year.

## Recommendation

Annual screening with low-dose computed tomography (LDCT) (grade B). The 2021 update lowered the starting age from 55 to 50 and the threshold from 30 to 20 pack-years.

## When to stop

Stop screening once a person has not smoked for 15 years, or if they develop a health problem that substantially limits life expectancy or the ability or willingness to have curative lung surgery.

## Notes

Screening should be combined with counseling and support to quit smoking for anyone who still smokes.
