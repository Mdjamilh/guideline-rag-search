## Who it applies to

Adults aged 40 and older without a known diagnosis of osteoporosis or a history of fragility fractures.

## Recommendation

- Women aged 65 and older: screen for osteoporosis with bone measurement testing (grade B).
- Postmenopausal women younger than 65 who are at increased risk, as determined by a formal clinical risk assessment tool such as FRAX: screen with bone measurement testing (grade B).
- Men: the evidence is insufficient to assess screening (I statement).

## Tests

Dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA) of the hip and lumbar spine is the most commonly used test.
