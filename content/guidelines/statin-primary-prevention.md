## Who it applies to

Adults aged 40 to 75 without a history of cardiovascular disease (such as heart attack or stroke) and without very high LDL cholesterol (190 mg/dL or higher) or familial hypercholesterolemia.

## Recommendation

- Prescribe a statin for adults aged 40 to 75 who have at least one cardiovascular risk factor (dyslipidemia, diabetes, hypertension or smoking) and an estimated 10-year cardiovascular risk of 10% or higher (grade B).
- Selectively offer a statin to adults aged 40 to 75 with at least one risk factor and an estimated 10-year risk of 7.5% to less than 10% (grade C).

## Older adults

The evidence is insufficient to assess benefits and harms of starting a statin in adults aged 76 and older (I statement).

## Notes

Risk is usually estimated with the pooled cohort equations. Shared decision-making about benefits, harms and preferences is encouraged.
