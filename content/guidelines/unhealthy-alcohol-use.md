## Who it applies to

Adults aged 18 and older, including pregnant people.

## Recommendation

Screen for unhealthy alcohol use in primary care, and provide brief behavioral counseling to people engaged in risky or hazardous drinking (grade B).

## Tools

Brief validated screening tools include the AUDIT-C and the single-question screen (for example, "How many times in the past year have you had 5 or more drinks in a day?" for men, or 4 or more for women).

## Adolescents

For adolescents aged 12 to 17, the evidence is insufficient (I statement).
