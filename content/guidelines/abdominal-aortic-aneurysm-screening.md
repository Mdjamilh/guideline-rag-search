## Who it applies to

Adults aged 65 to 75 without symptoms.

## Recommendation

- Men aged 65 to 75 who have ever smoked: one-time screening with abdominal ultrasound (grade B). "Ever smoked" generally means 100 or more cigarettes in a lifetime.
- Men aged 65 to 75 who have never smoked: offer screening selectively based on medical history, family history and other risk factors (grade C).
- Women who have never smoked and have no family history of aneurysm: the Task Force recommends against routine screening (grade D).
- Women aged 65 to 75 who have ever smoked or have a family history: the evidence is insufficient (I statement).

## Notes

A single ultrasound is usually enough; repeat screening is not routinely recommended when the first result is normal.
