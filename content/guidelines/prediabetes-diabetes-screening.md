## Who it applies to

Adults aged 35 to 70 who have overweight (BMI 25 or higher) or obesity and no symptoms of diabetes.

## Recommendation

Screen for prediabetes and type 2 diabetes (grade B). People found to have prediabetes should be offered or referred to effective preventive interventions, such as intensive lifestyle programs focused on diet and physical activity.

## Tests and interval

Screening can use fasting plasma glucose, HbA1c or an oral glucose tolerance test. Based on limited evidence, screening every 3 years may be reasonable for adults with normal results.

## Notes

Clinicians should consider screening at an earlier age, or at a lower BMI, for people from groups with a higher prevalence of diabetes or with other risk factors such as a family history or a history of gestational diabetes.
